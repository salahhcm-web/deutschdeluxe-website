Ask on WhatsApp
I’m a complete beginner. Where do I start?
Start with A1. Beginner groups start every few weeks with morning, evening and weekend options. Take the free placement test or message us and we will confirm the next start date.
How long does it take to reach B1?
Each level (A1, A2, B1) takes roughly 8–10 weeks in a standard group, so most learners reach B1 in about 6–8 months. Intensive groups move faster.
Do you prepare for Goethe, ÖSD and telc exams?
Yes — exam bootcamps for Goethe-Zertifikat, ÖSD and telc from A1 to C1, with timed mock exams and speaking simulations with a trainer. Exams are taken at the official exam centers in Cairo; we guide you through registration.
How much do courses cost?
Each level has a fixed fee that includes materials and portal access. Installments and A1+A2 bundles are available. See the courses page for current prices — confirmed by our team when you book.
Where exactly are you located?
Nasr City, Cairo. Open the map link below or on the contact page, or message us for directions and a visit.
Are there online or hybrid options?
Groups meet in person in Nasr City; some levels also run as live online groups. Every student gets the digital student portal with materials, progress tracking and notifications.
