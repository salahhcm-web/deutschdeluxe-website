Which exam?

Same levels.
Different doors.

All three are recognised by German authorities for visas, Ausbildung, universities and Approbation. The right one depends on your deadline, budget and which center has a free date in Cairo.

Comparison of Goethe, ÖSD and telc exams
Exam Levels Typically used for Format notes Where in Egypt
Goethe-Zertifikat A1 – C2 Family-reunion visa (A1), Ausbildung (B1), university (C1/C2), Approbation (B2) Four modules (Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, Sprechen) that can be taken and repeated separately from B1 upward Goethe-Institut Kairo (Dokki) and Alexandria
ÖSD A1 – C2 Austria & Germany visas, Ausbildung, nursing recognition, universities Written and oral parts; ÖSD ZB1 is modular. Austrian, German and Swiss standard German in listening Licensed ÖSD exam centers in Cairo (dates vary)
telc A1 – C1 Ausbildung & work (B1/B2), telc B2·C1 Medizin / Pflege for doctors and nurses, C1 Hochschule Profession-specific exams (Medizin, Pflege, Beruf); written and oral on one or two days Licensed telc centers in Cairo (dates vary)

Exam fees are paid directly to the exam center and are not included in our course fees. Always confirm the recognition requirement with the embassy, university or Landesprüfungsamt you are applying to.

Ask about the FSP track ↗
Doctors, dentists, pharmacists & nurses

Medical German
& Fachsprachprüfung.

Approbation needs B2 general German plus the Fachsprachprüfung (C1 medical). Nursing recognition needs B2 (often telc B2 Pflege). We run this as one continuous track so you never lose momentum between levels.

Plan my medical track ↗

B2 medical module

Anatomy and symptom vocabulary, patient-centred communication, taking a history, explaining diagnoses in plain German.

FSP simulation

The three FSP parts under time: Arzt-Patienten-Gespräch (20 min), Dokumentation / Arztbrief (20 min), Arzt-Arzt-Gespräch (20 min).
