About Deutsch Deluxe

Made in Cairo.
Built for your next chapter.

We are a German language training center in Nasr City with one job: to get ambitious Egyptians from “Hallo” to the certificate that changes their plans — and to make the road there human, structured and even fun.

Our story

Why we exist.

Deutsch Deluxe started with a small group of learners around one table in Nasr City and a simple frustration: German courses in Cairo were either impersonal and huge, or friendly but unstructured. Nobody told you what your level was, when your group would start or what you needed for the visa.

So we built the school we wished existed: groups capped at twelve, trainers who are certified examiners, a fixed calendar of start dates, transparent prices — and a digital student portal that shows every learner exactly where they stand.

Today our learners include doctors preparing for Approbation, engineers heading for Ausbildung, students bound for German universities, parents who want their kids to grow up bilingual, and people who simply fell in love with the language. Different plans, one address.

Our method

Four skills.
Every single session.

Certificates test all four skills, and so does real life in Germany. We never let a session become a grammar lecture — you speak from minute one.

Sprechen — Speaking

Pair and group tasks, role-plays and weekly Stammtisch nights. Pronunciation coaching with native-speaker input. You talk more than the trainer.

Hören — Listening

Authentic audio from day one — announcements, podcasts, dialects — plus exam-style listening tasks so nothing surprises you.

Lesen — Reading

From menus and job ads to newspaper articles and academic texts. Strategies for speed and for the traps exam writers love.

Schreiben — Writing

Emails, complaints, applications, essays and Arztbriefe — corrected individually within 48 hours through the portal.

Small groups, real attention

Max. 12 learners (8 at C1). Your trainer knows your name, your goal and your weak spots by week two.

Goal before grammar

We start with where you are going — visa, university, hospital — and design the path backwards from that deadline.

Structure you can see

Fixed start dates, a syllabus per level, weekly quizzes and a portal that tracks it all. No mystery, no lost months.

Certificates

What you take home.

At the end of each level you sit a mock exam in the format of Goethe / ÖSD / telc and receive a Deutsch Deluxe certificate of completion with your level, hours and skills profile.

For official recognition (visas, universities, Approbation) you take the international exam at a licensed center in Cairo. We are not an exam center ourselves — we are the place where you become ready for one, and we guide you through registration.
