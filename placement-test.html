Find your level

15 quick questions from A1 to B1. You get your level and a recommended course instantly — then send the result to our team and we confirm the next start date.

Before you start

How the test works

  • 15 multiple-choice questions: 5 at A1, 5 at A2, 5 at B1 — grammar and vocabulary in context
  • About 5 minutes. No dictionary, no guessing — if you don’t know, pick the answer that feels most natural
  • Your result and recommended course appear immediately; nothing is saved on this website
  • Complete beginner? Skip the test — you belong in A1. Ask about the next A1 group ↗
After the test

From result to
your first session.

The online test measures grammar and vocabulary. Speaking is where levels are really decided — so we always add a short, friendly speaking check.

Send your result

One tap sends your score and details to our WhatsApp. We reply during opening hours, usually within minutes.

10-minute speaking check

A trainer chats with you in German (or listens while you try) — in the center or on a WhatsApp call. Free, no preparation needed.

Your group & start date

You get a confirmed level, the next groups at your level with dates, prices and seats — and a 48-hour seat hold.
